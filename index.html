We grew tired of "Top 10 Languages to Learn in 2024" videos. They don't know you. They don't know your cognitive strengths or what makes you lose track of time while coding.

Pathified was born out of a simple frustration: the transition from student to specialized professional is broken. We use psychometrics and engineering market data to build the bridge.
