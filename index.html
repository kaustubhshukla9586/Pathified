Why Pathified

Built because everyone had advice.
Nobody had answers.

There's no shortage of people telling CS students what to do. Watch this video. Learn this language. Follow this roadmap. But none of it is built for you specifically — it's built for everyone, which means it's built for no one.

The problem is real. When you're surrounded by noise and can't figure out your direction, it doesn't feel like an information problem. It feels personal. Like maybe you're the one who's broken.

Pathified exists because AI can actually listen to who you are — not just what you've learned. Twenty-five questions that adapt to your answers, and a result that was built specifically around you.
